What to do first: migration roadmap

Glossary

  • Shor's algorithm: a quantum algorithm that breaks RSA, Diffie-Hellman and elliptic curves.
  • Grover's algorithm: a quantum search that roughly halves the strength of symmetric keys (AES-128 becomes about 64-bit).
  • ML-KEM / ML-DSA / SLH-DSA: NIST's post-quantum standards FIPS 203, 204 and 205 (August 2024).
  • Heuristic: matched by a configuration pattern, not by parsing; confirm by hand.
